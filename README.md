# Ramya Akula, Ph.D.

### AI Research Scientist | Trustworthy AI · NLP · Computational Biology

This repository hosts my professional research portfolio.

My work focuses on developing and evaluating interpretable and reliable machine-learning methods for complex scientific and human-centered problems. My research spans **trustworthy and interpretable AI, natural language processing, language evaluation, and computational biology**.
