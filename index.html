Ramya Akula, Ph.D.

Machine Learning Researcher  •  Trustworthy AI  •  Computational Biology  •  NLP
San Jose, California, USA

I develop trustworthy machine learning methods for understanding complex biological systems and human language, with the long-term goal of improving the reliability of AI in healthcare. My work focuses on a central question: "How can we build AI systems that are reliable enough to support decisions when mistakes matter?"

As a 2022 DARPA Forward Riser, my research on interpretable self-attention models for figurative language detection explored how machine learning systems reason under semantic ambiguity. During my postdoctoral research at The Ohio State University (Dept. of Internal Medicine, Pulmonary), I developed scalable computational pipelines for integrating multi-cohort single-cell transcriptomic datasets to study lung aging and cellular senescence.

My future work targets trustworthy clinical AI — evaluation of conversational systems, adversarial testing of LLMs, and uncertainty communication — integrating ML, NLP, and computational biology.
